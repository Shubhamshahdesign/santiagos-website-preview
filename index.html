About us

A neighbourhood cafe in the oldest corner of the city.

Santiago's sits on Cannon Street, right beside St James Priory and a short walk from the Bristol Coach Station. Regulars come in for a cooked breakfast before work, travellers stop for a coffee and a slice of cake before their coach, and plenty of people simply pop in for a good, hot lunch.

We keep things simple. Food is cooked to order, plates come out hot, and the prices stay fair. If the ground floor looks full, head upstairs, there is more seating there.

  • Cooked to order, every time
  • Homemade cakes and bakes daily
  • Vegetarian and vegan breakfasts
  • Upstairs seating when it gets busy
